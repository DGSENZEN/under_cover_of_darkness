# Credits

## Sound

Every sound in the game is a recording, cut and levelled from the packs below by
`tools/prepare_sfx.py` into `audio/sfx/` and `audio/ambience/`.

### Attribution required

- **Metal footsteps** (`step_metal`): "Footsteps on different surfaces" by
  **congusbongus**, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces),
  licensed [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Derived from
  "boots on aluminum ladder 01" by **Eelke**
  ([freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Eelke/sounds/462598/)), CC-BY 3.0.
- **Stings** (`sting_suspicious`, `sting_combat`): "Orchestral Stinger - Dramatic
  Entrance" by **tcarisland**,
  [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/orchestral-stinger-dramatic-entrance),
  licensed [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cut into two
  (its opening swell, and the chord it lands on).
