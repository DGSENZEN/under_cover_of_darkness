# Combat exchange practice

Run `res://maps/combat_arena.tscn` for the proving grounds. The training yard has a fencer for feints and an arms master for defence and footwork. The ring and gauntlet test those choices under pressure. R rests and restores the encounter.

- **Change the question:** start a side cut with LMB, tap RMB during its windup to feint, release RMB, then flick up for a thrust or down for an overhead and click LMB again. A quick changed attack winds up faster. Feinting that follow-through does not renew the boost. Each feint still costs stamina.
- **Bait the blade:** let a nearby enemy commit at his glint, then step outside his arc or just beyond his reach. A genuine close miss ends his attack string and briefly exposes him. Stay close enough to answer; swinging safely across the room, hiding behind a wall, or standing above him earns no opening.
- **Punish the wasted parry:** draw the fencer's parry with a feint. Attack after his blade has met thin air. A quick reply into that opening hits harder, presses his posture and rocks him more decisively. Attacking while his parry is still active can still get you parried.
- **Read the contact:** crossed strokes at the reticle confirm flesh; blue brackets show stopped steel; an amber diamond confirms an earned punish or perfect deflect. A red diamond confirms a deathblow. These cues fade quickly. Small amber arcs over the enemy's posture bar mark a temporary punish opening; the filled red diamond still means his posture is broken.

An earned punish applies to one landed melee hit against the enemy you baited. It expires if you wait, and it gives no free stamina or universal riposte. Your committed swings still leave you vulnerable to mistakes.

Use the movement gym's combat / stealth relay (station 16, F6 sentry, F8 damage) to test these exchanges after a vault, mantle, climb or disengagement. Use R to restart each attempt.
