# The Revenant Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new monster you can put down but only fire keeps dead: its own body and rig, surges, swipes, a two-touch grasp ending in possession, the fire shell, then the chain brazier.

**Architecture:** `Revenant.gd extends Monster` holds the brain and the rules; `RevenantBody.gd` is presentation only (sprung poses and solved limbs, as `AnimaBody`/`AnimaMouth`). `RevenantTouch.gd` is a player child holding the touch, as `EvilEyeCurse` is. All numbers are a `Bestiary` row. The fire shell extends `Rounds`, `Loadout` and `Revolver`; the brazier extends `Weapon`, `PlayerCombat` and `Loadout`.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.5.1 GDScript, Blender headless scripts, `--fixed-fps 60` suites.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-revenant-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Failing checks first ([[failing-checks-kept]]). Suites pass on: exit 0, no `^FAIL`, no `SCRIPT ERROR`/`SHADER ERROR`, and every `^ERROR` read.
- Suite: `timeout 290 "$G" --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . res://tests/<name>.tscn`, with `G=/Users/tinkertailorr/Desktop/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot`.
- Anything on screen in words of ours is a stand-in, listed on `docs/superpowers/refs/hunt/words_needed.md`. The user writes all words.
- Effects are ours; sounds are stand-ins from approved packs, wanted takes on `sounds_needed.md` and `elevenlabs_prompts.md`.
- Nothing moved in `_process` where it would judder. A body moved while hidden calls `reset_physics_interpolation()`.
- First-person arm and hand poses are authored with the user in their live Blender, never computed and shown as finished.
- Never commit `assets/monsters/source/anima_hands.blend`. Use explicit file lists. Commit messages end `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Frame cost: its gym's worst wave holds 60 fps at 1152x648 with vsync on.
- **Three stops for the user's verdict are marked STOP. They are the spec's, and work does not pass them on its own.** Look at every joint and every moving shot before each ([[finished-means-meticulous]]).

## Review Focus

1. The one that laid the touch no longer exists in play (asleep, reloaded dead, wave reset): the touch must clear, never strand you one touch from death with nothing to burn. Test T9 in Task 5.
2. A second revenant grasps you the moment the first lets go: a kill you couldn't have answered. A short grace after the hold (`touch_grace`, Claude's addition to the spec) stops it. Test T6 in Task 5.
3. An empowered fire shell turns into `&"blast"`, or T cycles fire shells into the cylinder: the round must stay fire, and only in the shot barrel. Tests F6, F7 in Task 6.
4. Saved while it lies down, burning, or with the touch on you: it comes back as it was, not standing or at full health. Tests S1 to S3 in Task 8.
5. It falls on a slope, against a wall, or surges into one: the acted fall stays on the floor and it gets up where it can stand; a surge stops at the wall and doesn't push through it. Tests M4, M5 in Task 3.
