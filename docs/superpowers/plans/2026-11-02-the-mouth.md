# The Mouth Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the torso's stand-in boxes with the mouth: several ánimas fused into one, with its own movement, a cage that closes on you, and its own deaths.

**Architecture:** A fused ribcage-and-flesh mesh with a two-sided mouth rig, built by a Blender script from a CC BY skeleton's ribs. `AnimaBody` hangs the approved rigged hands and skull off markers in it, so they keep moving on their own. In first person a new `LeechCage` sits with `LeechHands` and `LeechFace`. The form stays `&"torso"` throughout.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.5.1, GDScript, Blender headless Python.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-the-mouth-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Failing checks first; run, read, then commit, never chained.
- `G=/Users/tinkertailorr/Desktop/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot`; a suite: `timeout 500 "$G" --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . res://tests/<name>.tscn`. Passing means exit 0, no `^FAIL`, no `SCRIPT ERROR`.
- Fight numbers stay: health 130, grip 2, power drain 12, life drain 4, swipe 14 damage at 2.2 m with a 0.6 s wind-up, speed 1.4, tell 0.95, leap reach 5, leap speed 8.5. Only `height` and `hit_radius` change.
- Every part is a closed solid; the script refuses an open edge. Nothing sawed off; every entry into the flesh sculpted.
- Stills from the real game, lit room and dark yard, each joint inspected close up, before anything is shown.
- No `juice.punch` pitch for anything repeating. No words of ours on screen. Stand-in sounds from approved packs; wanted takes on `sounds_needed.md` and `elevenlabs_prompts.md`.
- 60 fps at 1152x648 with vsync on in the worst wave, measured with `tests/diag_mixed_perf.tscn`.
- Commit with explicit file lists, ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- **Two stops for the user's verdict, the only ones:** Task 1's pictures and Task 2's in-game look. Nothing later is built before those verdicts.

## Review Focus

1. A mouth on you with a face and hands on you too, or two mouths: the slots and pieces stay sane, the middle stays clear, and each whip goes where it's aimed. (Task 4)
2. Whipped once, then killed, thrown by the Burst or the line, or torn off by a blink: no cracked ribs left on the next hold, no cage left on screen. (Task 4)
3. A mass this size at cover, at the gym's pinch, in a doorway: it gets round or through as the other forms do, and never pushes you through a wall in its lunge. (Task 3)
4. The Burst and the Shard on a mass with limbs on it (`jolt`, `tumble`, `flung_up`, `AnimaSpill`): it reacts, and its limbs stay on it. (Task 3)
5. Dying with the mouth on you beside a face: one last gulp, with no fight between the cage closing and the face coming in. (Task 5)
