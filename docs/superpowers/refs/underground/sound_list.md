# The Underground's sounds: what to source

The bay's first sounds were stand-ins cut from packs with no metro in them, and judged bad on
every count: too loud, fake, repetitive, dry (Oct 17 2026). These are the recordings that would
replace them. Field recordings beat designed ones; longer takes beat short loops (each is cut
into many random pieces, so a minute of one take gives a long stretch with no repeat). Drop them
anywhere in Downloads with their licence, and they will be cut by tools/prepare_sfx.py and
credited in CREDITS.md.

| # | What | Why (where it plays) | Ideal take |
|---|------|----------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Room tone of a tiled underground passage: distant activity, no clear words | the bed under everything in a corridor | stereo, 1-3 min |
| 2 | Crowd walla in a reverberant hall, indistinct talk and walking, sparse and busy | the corridor's people, nearer and farther | stereo, 1-2 min each density |
| 3 | Footsteps of passers-by on tile in a hall (not close-miked) | the walkers who pass the stall | several takes, mono or stereo |
| 4 | A train in a tunnel heard from a platform or passage: arriving, standing, leaving; and one heard through walls | the metro breathing every 90-150 s | 30-60 s each |
| 5 | Brake squeal and wheel screech on a curve | inside the train's pass | a few takes |
| 6 | Fluorescent or neon tube hum and buzz, close; a transformer or electrical box hum | her sign, the junction box | 30 s+ each |
| 7 | Water drips in a concrete or tiled space: single drips, and a slow drip | the conduit's weeping joint into the drain | 20+ single drips, 1 min loop |
| 8 | An air vent or grille breathing low | the vent in the back wall | 30 s+ |
| 9 | A steamer pot: low hiss, simmer, a lid lifted and set back | the tamale cart | 30 s+ hiss, a few lid takes |
| 10 | A long, low wind through a tunnel, hollow, no whistle | the dark train's cold draft | 30 s+ |
| 11 | A large dog: a low growl, slow breathing, claws on tile | the nahual | a few takes each |

Anything with spoken words (a PA, a vendor's call) only if you want those words; otherwise
none. CC0 is simplest; other licences are fine once you approve them.
