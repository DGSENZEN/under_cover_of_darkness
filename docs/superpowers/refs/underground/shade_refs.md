# The procession's shades: references (Oct 17 2026)

The user on the first procession: "the humanoid models genuinely do not look good, grab a
reference and then take it so the shades look much better." Anatomical bodies under thin clothes
read as mannequins. The references below agree on something else: a shade is a shroud with no body
in it.

## References
- **The Santa Compaña mural, Pontevedra** ([Wikimedia Commons, Lameiro, public domain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Santa+Compa%C3%B1a&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image)).
  Galicia's procession of the dead, with candles, the nearest kin of the Mexican ánimas' procession.
  - Tall pointed hoods; long shrouds falling straight to the ground.
  - Hollow dark faces (skulls glimpsed in the hood); candles before them.
  - Pale white-grey, glowing a little against the dark.
  - A painting of the same procession shows the shrouds translucent, the wall behind them showing
    through.
- **The Procesión del Silencio, San Luis Potosí** ([Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Procesi%C3%B3n+del+Silencio+San+Luis+Potos%C3%AD&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image)).
  Mexico's own hooded procession.
  - The capirote's tall cone; a cape hanging over the chest; robes to the floor.
  - The file walking in silence, carrying candles.
- **The procession of the ánimas** (the Mexican legend; supernatural_refs.md).
  - The dead in white, carrying candles.
- **Stylized ghosts in games** (the common ground of hooded spirit concepts).
  - The face lost in the hood's shadow; robes tapering, the feet hidden.
  - The hem ragged, trailing into mist.

## The shades, then
1. **A shroud, not a body.** One tall draped form from the hood to a ragged hem. No anatomy shows
   but the hands' hint at the candle.
2. **Hoods give the variety.**
   - The capirote's tall point (two of them).
   - A rebozo's rounded veil (a woman).
   - A small one (a child).
   - One with his sombrero over his shroud, so he is still himself. He is the ownerless shadow on
     the wall.
3. **The face a hollow:** Mignola's black, recessed into the hood. Nothing drawn in it.
4. **Drapery:** vertical folds that deepen toward the hem. A cape over the chest. Sleeves meeting
   before them where the hands hold the candle.
5. **The hem ragged and lifted off the floor:** they float, and mist tendrils trail from the hem
   (MeshVfx), the Deadlock way.
6. **The look** (shade.gdshader):
   - Pale, unlit, translucent in the middle and bright at the rim (fresnel), as the painted
     shrouds glow.
   - A slow noise climbing them.
   - Eaten at the hem.
   - Their candles' warm light the only colour on them.
